# OserAI code of conduct

OserAI welcomes people testing their first local model, sharing hardware
results, writing documentation, and contributing code. These expectations
apply in project issues, discussions, reviews, and other spaces where people
participate on behalf of OserAI.

## How we work together

- Treat people with respect, including when you disagree.
- Give feedback on the work and explain what you observed.
- Make room for newcomers and questions about setup, hardware, and terminology.
- Respect people's privacy when sharing logs, screenshots, or conversations.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, unwanted sexual attention, and
sharing someone else's private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

For abuse on GitHub, [report it to GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse)
and include a link to the relevant content. GitHub's reporting tools also let
you report eligible issues, discussions, and comments from their menus.

Project maintainers may remove harmful content, close or lock conversations,
and restrict participation according to the circumstances.
